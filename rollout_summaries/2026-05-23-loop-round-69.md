## Round 67 — `SAOPlayerGUIDamageEventsMixin`

The damage-event normalization + pending-combat-reset cluster.
These are called from the packet damage callback path to:
1. defer DPS encounter resets until the next real player-vs-monster
   hit (so a scene change doesn't blank the bar mid-fight),
2. normalize incoming damage events so SELF / friendly / enemy
   attribution is consistent regardless of which packet variant
   the bridge emitted.

5 methods, ~287 lines net:

| Method | Lines | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `_maybe_apply_pending_combat_reset` | 88 | Pending DPS reset gate — applies only on real self-combat hits |
| `_current_player_uid_int` | 16 | Best-effort SELF UID fetch |
| `_is_known_friendly_uid` | 42 | Predicate for in-session player roster |
| `_normalize_damage_event_for_self` | 47 | Patches `attacker_is_self` + cleans stale SELF UID |
| `_normalize_damage_event_target_for_entity` | 94 | Rewrites target attribution for entity HUD's Boss-HP lock |

Mixin: 341 lines (header + 60-line docstring + bodies). Imports:
`time` only.

sao_gui.py: 1015 → 729 (-286).
